In collaboration with scientists at Vertex Pharmacueticals, we are using the
polarizable AMOEBA force field evaluated using Ewald summation to re-refine
protein-drug structures in the context of the original diffraction data (ie. using
an X-ray likelihood target) in order to provide the most precise atomic
resolution models possible.

Compared to the original models, the Ewald refinements exhibit:

. Improved agreement with the data (lower R~free~ values)
. Fewer van der Waals clashes
. A greater percentage of favorable Ramachandran backbone conformations
. Overall improvement of MolProbity score
. Refinement of the hydrogen-bonding network within the binding pocket
